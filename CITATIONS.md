# Citations

Please cite the tools seq-resistome runs.

- **CARD and RGI**: Alcock BP, et al. CARD 2023: expanded curation, support
  for machine learning, and resistome prediction at the Comprehensive
  Antibiotic Resistance Database. *Nucleic Acids Research* 51:D690-D699
  (2023). doi:10.1093/nar/gkac920
- **KMA** (RGI bwt's aligner): Clausen PTLC, Aarestrup FM, Lund O. Rapid and
  precise alignment of raw reads against redundant databases with KMA.
  *BMC Bioinformatics* 19:307 (2018). doi:10.1186/s12859-018-2336-6
- **fastp**: Chen S, Zhou Y, Chen Y, Gu J. fastp: an ultra-fast all-in-one
  FASTQ preprocessor. *Bioinformatics* 34:i884-i890 (2018).
  doi:10.1093/bioinformatics/bty560
- **hostile**: Constantinides B, Hunt M, Crook DW. Hostile: accurate
  decontamination of microbial host sequences. *Bioinformatics* 39:btad728
  (2023). doi:10.1093/bioinformatics/btad728
- **Bowtie 2**: Langmead B, Salzberg SL. Fast gapped-read alignment with
  Bowtie 2. *Nature Methods* 9:357-359 (2012). doi:10.1038/nmeth.1923
- **SortMeRNA**: Kopylova E, Noé L, Touzet H. SortMeRNA: fast and accurate
  filtering of ribosomal RNAs in metatranscriptomic data. *Bioinformatics*
  28:3211-3217 (2012). doi:10.1093/bioinformatics/bts611
- **SILVA**: Quast C, et al. The SILVA ribosomal RNA gene database project:
  improved data processing and web-based tools. *Nucleic Acids Research*
  41:D590-D596 (2013). doi:10.1093/nar/gks1219
- **nf-core**: Ewels PA, et al. The nf-core framework for community-curated
  bioinformatics pipelines. *Nature Biotechnology* 38:276-278 (2020).
  doi:10.1038/s41587-020-0439-x
- **Nextflow**: Di Tommaso P, et al. Nextflow enables reproducible
  computational workflows. *Nature Biotechnology* 35:316-319 (2017).
  doi:10.1038/nbt.3820

Validation only (not run by the pipeline): ART (Huang W, et al.
*Bioinformatics* 28:593-594, 2012; doi:10.1093/bioinformatics/btr708) and
barrnap (Seemann T, https://github.com/tseemann/barrnap).
